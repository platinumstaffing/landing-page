# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- **Employers / hiring decision-makers** (operations managers, HR, plant and warehouse
  leads across Pennsylvania manufacturing, warehousing, distribution, logistics,
  administrative support, customer service, and light industrial). Their context: they are
  evaluating whether to trust a staffing partner with a real workforce gap. The job to be
  done is to solve that gap with someone dependable and to start a conversation (Request
  Talent, Schedule a Consultation).
- **Job seekers / professionals** looking for meaningful, stable work. Their job to be done
  is to find and apply to roles and to feel welcomed and respected, not "processed."

## Product Purpose

Position Platinum Staffing & Recruitment as a strategic workforce solutions partner, not
"another staffing agency." The website is the company's strongest business-development
asset: it builds employer trust, generates qualified inquiries, attracts candidates, and
reinforces credibility since 2019. Success is measured in employer inquiries and candidate
applications, not page views. Every page answers one question: "How does Platinum Staffing
help my business succeed?" (or, for candidates, "How does Platinum help my career?").

## Brand Personality

Established, dependable, precise, human, industrial-editorial. Professional without feeling
corporate; modern without appearing trendy; confident without being overwhelming. Employers
should feel Platinum understands their workforce challenges and can deliver. Job seekers
should feel welcomed, respected, and supported.

## Anti-references

- The generic "AI SaaS" look: default Inter body type, purple-to-blue gradients, neon glow,
  glassmorphism everywhere, 3D blobs.
- Stock handshakes, staged boardrooms, generic smiling headshots, futuristic or AI-generated
  imagery.
- Icon-in-a-circle on every card, an icon above every heading, bento grids without reason,
  marquees, parallax, cursor effects.
- Fake logos, fake stats, fake testimonials, or any fabricated content.
- Component-library defaults left unchanged.

## Design Principles

1. **Partner, not vendor.** Every page answers "how does Platinum help my business or career
   succeed?" Copy and layout lead with the visitor's outcome, not the company's features.
2. **Signature restraint.** Executive Purple is a deliberate signature, never a wash.
   Hairlines and flat navy structural bands carry rhythm over decoration.
3. **Industrial-editorial hierarchy.** Eyebrow, heading, lede, content. Avoid centering
   everything; let the layout read like considered editorial, not a template.
4. **Earned trust.** Never fabricate content. Unknown data lives in CONTENT_GAPS, not in
   invented client names, placements, or awards.
5. **Motion supports comprehension.** Confident and restrained; it never delays reading and
   always respects reduced-motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA; accessibility is not a cleanup task. Full `prefers-reduced-motion`
support (transforms disabled globally). Labels always visible, form errors announced, purple
focus rings on all interactive elements. Photography and illustration should represent real,
diverse, safety-aware workplaces in the industries served.

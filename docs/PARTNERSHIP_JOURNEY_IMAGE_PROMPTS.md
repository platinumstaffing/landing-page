# Partnership Journey Image Direction

## Purpose

These five illustrations replace the abstract SVG vignettes inside the “How the
partnership works” cards. They should make each step understandable before the visitor
opens its description, while reading together as one continuous workforce journey.

The system is diagrammatic rather than photographic. At the small card size, one clear
action, a small number of objects, and a consistent route motif will communicate more
effectively than miniature workplace scenes.

## Recommended visual language

**Concept:** A workforce route moving through five operational workstations.

**Style:** Premium editorial infographic with the precision of a production manual and the
warmth of cut-paper illustration. Flat shapes, restrained dimensional layering, crisp
hairlines, subtle paper grain, and confident negative space. Not isometric software art,
not cartoon characters, and not a generic corporate vector pack.

**Palette:**

- Soft White background: `#f8f9fb`
- Deep Navy primary structures: `#1e2a44`
- Charcoal details: `#2c2c2c`
- Platinum Silver secondary structures: `#c7ccd6`
- Executive Purple journey line and active waypoint only: `#4b2e83`
- No additional accent colors

**Shared journey motif:** A single 5–7 px Executive Purple route enters from the exact
middle of the left edge, travels through the central action, touches one solid circular
waypoint, and exits from the exact middle of the right edge. Keep both edge connections
unobstructed. The route may curve, turn, or loop inside each frame, but it must retain the
same stroke weight and visual character across all five images.

**People:** When a person is necessary, use a simplified adult figure built from editorial
shapes with believable proportions and practical workplace clothing. Show no facial
features, demographic stereotypes, branded uniforms, or decorative character poses.

**Output for every image:**

- Generate as a separate image, never as a five-panel collage.
- 1600 × 1200 pixels, 4:3 landscape.
- Keep the essential action inside the central 70% safe area.
- Soft White background, or transparent background if the generator produces clean alpha.
- No baked-in frame, card border, number, title, caption, button, or typography.
- Export final production assets as WebP and retain a high-resolution PNG source.

## Shared negative prompt

> Text, letters, numbers, captions, labels, UI screens, dashboards, logos, brand marks,
> gradients, glass effects, neon glow, 3D glossy rendering, generic SaaS illustration,
> childish cartoon style, stock vector people, handshakes, finish flags, trophy imagery,
> puzzle pieces, target icons, light bulbs, rocket ships, floating icons, decorative
> confetti, excessive arrows, complex flowcharts, crowded scenes, more than five primary
> objects, unsafe workplace behavior, distorted bodies or hands.

## 01. Discovery Consultation

**Message to convey**

Platinum listens first. Information from the employer’s operation, hiring need, schedule,
and culture is gathered into one shared understanding.

**Composition**

Use three quiet inputs on the left: a simplified factory-floor block, a shift-calendar
block, and a small team grouping. They should not look like floating icons; arrange them
as physical cards or paper artifacts sitting on one work surface. In the center, show two
simplified professionals leaning toward an open planning sheet from opposite sides. The
purple route enters from the left, passes by the three operational inputs, circles the
shared planning sheet once, touches a solid waypoint on the sheet, and exits at the middle
right.

The planning sheet is the focal point. The people support the act of listening and
understanding rather than becoming portraits. Preserve generous negative space above the
work surface.

**Generation prompt**

> Create one premium editorial infographic illustration for the Discovery Consultation
> stage of an employer staffing journey. Use a Soft White paper-like background, Deep Navy
> structural shapes, Charcoal details, Platinum Silver hairlines, and Executive Purple
> only for one continuous journey route and one waypoint. Arrange a simplified
> factory-floor block, a shift-calendar artifact, and a small team grouping on the left
> side of one practical work surface. At the center, two simplified adult professionals in
> practical workplace clothing lean toward the same open planning sheet from opposite
> sides, communicating attentive listening and a shared understanding rather than a sales
> meeting. The purple route enters at the exact middle of the left edge, moves past the
> three operational inputs, forms one calm loop around the shared planning sheet, touches
> a solid circular waypoint on the sheet, and exits at the exact middle of the right edge.
> Flat editorial shapes, restrained cut-paper depth, crisp production-manual hairlines,
> subtle paper grain, believable proportions, spacious composition, central action inside
> the middle 70 percent, 4:3 landscape, no text.

**Step-specific negative prompt**

> Handshake, contract signing, speech bubbles, sales presentation, boardroom, laptop
> meeting, interrogation, celebration, camera-facing people.

**Alt-text draft**

> An employer and staffing partner gathering operational needs around a shared plan.

**Filename**

`partnership-01-discovery.webp`

## 02. Workforce Strategy

**Message to convey**

The information gathered in discovery becomes a practical staffing model tied to roles,
timing, and workforce demand.

**Composition**

Show a horizontal planning table with three staggered shift blocks and a small set of role
tokens. Several thin Silver routes may begin as options, but only one Executive Purple
route should be selected. That route moves through the relevant shift blocks, gathers the
required role tokens, and arrives at one clear plan sheet or blueprint on the right-center.

The image should communicate selection and sequencing, not analytics. Use no graphs,
percentages, dashboards, or floating charts.

**Generation prompt**

> Create one premium editorial infographic illustration for the Workforce Strategy stage
> of an employer staffing journey. Use a Soft White paper-like background, Deep Navy
> planning structures, Charcoal details, Platinum Silver hairlines, and Executive Purple
> only for the selected route and one waypoint. Show a practical horizontal planning table
> containing three staggered shift blocks and a restrained set of role tokens. Introduce
> two or three very thin Silver route options near the left, then make one Executive
> Purple route clearly become the chosen path. The purple route enters at the exact middle
> of the left edge, moves through the appropriate shift blocks, gathers the necessary role
> tokens into an orderly sequence, touches one solid circular waypoint on a finished
> workforce plan sheet at the right-center, and exits at the exact middle of the right
> edge. The central visual idea is a tailored route becoming an actionable plan. Flat
> editorial shapes, subtle cut-paper depth, precise production-manual construction, quiet
> paper grain, generous negative space, no software interface, central action inside the
> middle 70 percent, 4:3 landscape, no text.

**Step-specific negative prompt**

> Analytics dashboard, pie chart, bar chart, fake data, gears, chess pieces, maze,
> complicated decision tree, glowing route, futuristic interface.

**Alt-text draft**

> A selected workforce route organizing roles and shifts into a practical staffing plan.

**Filename**

`partnership-02-strategy.webp`

## 03. Candidate Recruitment and Screening

**Message to convey**

Potential candidates move through a careful, human review process. The goal is qualified
alignment, not simply volume.

**Composition**

Begin with four simplified candidate records on the left, each represented by a small
profile sheet with a different arrangement of neutral experience marks. The purple route
guides them through three physical review stations: résumé review, conversation, and
qualification check. Avoid funnels, conveyor belts, or anything that treats people as
products. At the right-center, two aligned candidate records continue forward while the
others remain respectfully organized at the review station.

The waypoint should sit at the final qualification check. Keep the selected candidates
visually human and dignified even though the illustration is abstract.

**Generation prompt**

> Create one premium editorial infographic illustration for the Candidate Recruitment and
> Screening stage of an employer staffing journey. Use a Soft White paper-like background,
> Deep Navy structural shapes, Charcoal details, Platinum Silver hairlines, and Executive
> Purple only for one continuous journey route and one waypoint. Begin with four distinct
> candidate records arranged respectfully on the left, each shown as a small profile sheet
> with neutral experience marks and no facial photograph. Guide the records through three
> tactile, human review stations that suggest résumé review, a recruiter conversation, and
> a qualification check without using text or software screens. Do not use a funnel or
> conveyor belt. The purple route enters at the exact middle of the left edge, moves calmly
> through the three review stations, touches one solid circular waypoint at the final
> qualification check, and carries two clearly aligned candidate records toward the exact
> middle of the right edge. The other records remain neatly and respectfully organized,
> not rejected or discarded. Flat editorial shapes, restrained cut-paper dimension,
> production-manual precision, subtle paper grain, dignified human-centered tone, spacious
> 4:3 landscape composition, essential action inside the middle 70 percent, no text.

**Step-specific negative prompt**

> Funnel, conveyor belt, assembly line of people, red rejection marks, trash bin, ranking

**Alt-text draft**

## 04. Placement

**Message to convey**

A qualified candidate is introduced into the right role and becomes part of the employer’s
operation with a clear, supported handoff.

**Composition**

Place one selected candidate figure or record on the left-center and a simplified workplace
team station on the right-center. Between them, use a short bridge, docking rail, or aligned
threshold. The purple route should carry the candidate across that threshold and snap into
one intentionally open position within the team arrangement. Use a Silver secondary line
from the staffing partner to the threshold to suggest a supported handoff without adding
another person-heavy scene.

Do not use a checkmark as the main idea. The visual should show fit through alignment of
shapes and position.

**Generation prompt**

> Create one premium editorial infographic illustration for the Placement stage of an
> employer staffing journey. Use a Soft White paper-like background, Deep Navy workplace
> structures, Charcoal details, Platinum Silver hairlines, and Executive Purple only for
> one continuous journey route and one waypoint. Show one selected, simplified candidate
> figure or candidate record at the left-center and a practical workplace team station at
> the right-center with one intentionally open position. Connect them with a short,
> carefully aligned bridge or docking threshold. The purple route enters at the exact
> middle of the left edge, supports the candidate across the threshold, touches one solid
> circular waypoint at the moment of handoff, then carries the candidate into the open
> position so the shapes visibly align and become part of one team system. Add one quiet
> Platinum Silver support line leading to the handoff, suggesting recruiter coordination
> without clutter. Flat editorial shapes, restrained cut-paper dimension, precise
> production-manual construction, subtle paper grain, confident but non-celebratory tone,
> spacious 4:3 landscape composition, essential action inside the middle 70 percent, no
> text.

**Step-specific negative prompt**

> Handshake, giant checkmark, puzzle piece, trophy, finish line, podium, cheering team,
> employee badge close-up, contract signing, confetti.

**Alt-text draft**

> A qualified candidate crossing a supported handoff into an open position on a workplace
> team.

**Filename**

`partnership-04-placement.webp`

## 05. Ongoing Partnership

**Message to convey**

Placement is not the end. Platinum continues to check in, support the workforce, and help
the employer prepare for future needs.

**Composition**

Show the placed team station at the center with three quiet orbiting touchpoints:
workplace check-in, workforce planning, and future hiring need. These should be physical
artifacts, not icons floating randomly. The purple route enters from the left, moves
through the team, forms an open loop through the three touchpoints, touches its waypoint at
the team, and exits to the right while still curving forward. The route must not close into
a static circle or terminate at a finish.

The final exit should feel like the beginning of another cycle, visually inviting the line
to continue beyond the fifth card.

**Generation prompt**

> Create one premium editorial infographic illustration for the Ongoing Partnership stage
> of an employer staffing journey. Use a Soft White paper-like background, Deep Navy
> workforce structures, Charcoal details, Platinum Silver hairlines, and Executive Purple
> only for one continuous journey route and one waypoint. Place a stable workplace team
> station at the center. Arrange three restrained, physical planning artifacts around it
> that suggest a workplace check-in, future workforce planning, and a new hiring need,
> without text or floating app icons. The purple route enters at the exact middle of the
> left edge, passes through the established team, touches one solid circular waypoint at
> the team station, then forms a generous open loop through the three support touchpoints.
> The loop must remain visibly open and the route must exit at the exact middle of the
> right edge while curving forward, communicating continuity rather than completion. Flat
> editorial shapes, subtle cut-paper depth, precise production-manual hairlines, quiet
> paper grain, calm dependable tone, spacious 4:3 landscape composition, essential action
> inside the middle 70 percent, no text.

**Step-specific negative prompt**

> Closed circular arrow, finish flag, handshake, customer-support headset, trophy,
> celebration, infinite symbol as the entire image, floating notification badges, software
> dashboard.

**Alt-text draft**

> An established workplace team connected to check-ins, planning, and future hiring
> support.

**Filename**

`partnership-05-ongoing.webp`

## Connector treatment in the website

The connector should remain a code-rendered SVG rather than a generated bitmap. This keeps
it crisp, responsive, animatable, and accessible.

The current short bridges should be replaced when the final card illustrations are added:

1. Begin the route outside the first card, approximately 4% from the section’s left edge.
2. Run one continuous path behind the entire five-card group and end approximately 4% from
   the right edge.
3. Let the path travel through the horizontal center of every card’s illustration area,
   matching the entry and exit points specified above.
4. Add a small waypoint at every card, with the active or hovered step using a solid Purple
   point and the other steps using Silver outlines.
5. Use broader, slower curves between cards. Each bridge should occupy most of the gap
   rather than appearing as a short loose thread.
6. Begin the line before Step 01 and continue it after Step 05 so the composition reads as
   a route through the cards, not decoration trapped between them.
7. On mobile, switch to one vertical route running behind the left third of the stacked
   cards. Each card receives a short horizontal branch into its illustration.
8. Under reduced motion, render the complete route immediately. Otherwise, draw it once as
   the journey enters the viewport.

## Review checklist

- Can a visitor identify the action of each step without reading the modal?
- Does the Purple route enter and exit at exactly the same height in every image?
- Does each frame contain one dominant action rather than a collection of symbols?
- Are people treated as participants rather than units moving through machinery?
- Does Step 05 visibly continue forward instead of showing an ending?
- Is all essential detail legible when the image is displayed around 180–220 pixels wide?
- Are there no embedded words, fake data, logos, or software interfaces?
- Does Executive Purple remain limited to the route and waypoint?
